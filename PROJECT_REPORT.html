1. Abstract

Nano-aquariums (enclosures typically holding under 40 litres of water) present severe ecological challenges due to their constrained water volume: trace biochemical fluctuations escalate rapidly into lethal toxicity spikes. The leading cause of hobbyist fish mortality is "New Tank Syndrome"—the catastrophic accumulation of un-ionized ammonia (NH₃) and nitrite (NO₂⁻) prior to the full colonization of nitrifying autotrophic bacteria. Existing commercial tracking software relies heavily on passive manual logging with zero predictive foresight, static threshold comparisons, and no automated diagnostic capabilities.

This project presents AquaPulse, a production-grade, desktop-based data analytics and monitoring system developed in Python using the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architectural pattern. AquaPulse combines an enterprise-grade local SQLite3 (Write-Ahead Logging) relational storage layer with Pandas and NumPy computational backends. The system introduces three key engineering innovations:

  • Mass-Balance Dilution Engine: Computes exact restorative water change volumes based on conservation of mass principles (C₁V₁ + C₂V₂ = C_fV_f);
  • Predictive Maintenance Module: Utilizes discrete gradient approximations (d[NO₃⁻]/dt) over rolling historical data to compute a real-time countdown to threshold breach; and
  • Rule-Based NLP Veterinary Expert System: Parses colloquial symptom descriptions, weights diagnosis confidence using species vulnerability multipliers across 10 aquatic species, and returns clinical, dosage-accurate pharmaceutical regimens.

2. Introduction & Background

2.1 The Nitrogen Cycle in Closed Biomes

In an enclosed artificial aquatic environment, biological equilibrium depends entirely on the Nitrogen Cycle, a two-step autotrophic bacterial oxidation process:

  1. Ammonification: Aquatic livestock excrete nitrogenous waste primarily as ammonia (NH₃/NH₄⁺) across their gill membranes and through biological breakdown of organic debris.
  2. Nitrosation: Chemolithotrophic bacteria (primarily Nitrosomonas) oxidize toxic ammonia into intermediate nitrite:
    2 NH₃ + 3 O₂ ➔ 2 NO₂⁻ + 2 H⁺ + 2 H₂O
  3. Nitratation: Distinct bacterial strains (Nitrobacter and Nitrospira) oxidize nitrite into nitrate:
    2 NO₂⁻ + O₂ ➔ 2 NO₃⁻

Unlike natural open ecosystems with continuous rain and anaerobic denitrification, an enclosed aquarium lacks natural mechanisms to purge nitrate. Nitrate accumulates steadily, inducing chronic immunosuppression and mortality unless exported through regular, calculated manual water exchanges.

4. Mathematical Formulations

4.1 Composite Toxicity Index

Rather than treating water parameters in isolation, AquaPulse evaluates an empirical composite toxicity score:

T_index = 0.40 · s(NH₃) + 0.30 · s(NO₂⁻) + 0.15 · s(NO₃⁻) + 0.10 · s(pH) + 0.05 · s(Temp)

Weights are assigned according to acute biological hazard, where un-ionized ammonia and nitrite are heavily prioritized over nitrate and temperature fluctuations.

4.2 Exact Mass-Balance Dilution Equation

When toxicity spikes, the application calculates the exact percentage water dilution required without over-shocking the inhabitants:

ΔV% = (1 - C_target / C_initial) × 100%

Recommendations are dynamically bounded between 15% and 70% to prevent severe osmotic shock.

4.3 Rate of Accumulation & Predictive Maintenance

Using NumPy's discrete central-difference gradient over historical readings, the application computes the rate of accumulation:

r = d[NO₃⁻] / dt ≈ np.gradient(nitrate_series, time_steps)
Days Remaining = ⌈ ([NO₃⁻]_threshold - [NO₃⁻]_current) / r ⌉

6. Conclusion & Future Scope

AquaPulse proves that applying solid software design patterns (MVC) and data engineering techniques to biological micro-habitats creates an intuitive, life-saving application. It eliminates guesswork for hobbyists and elevates aquarium maintenance from reactive panic to proactive mathematical control.
